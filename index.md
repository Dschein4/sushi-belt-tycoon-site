# Privacy Policy — Sushi Rush

**Effective date: October 13, 2026** · Contact: **contact.weastcoastgames@gmail.com**

Sushi Rush ("the app") is developed by **Weast Coast Games** ("we").
This policy explains what data is handled when you play.

## Data we collect directly
None. The app requires no account, and your game progress is saved only on
your device. We never see your name, email, contacts, photos, or files.

## Data collected by third-party services

**Gameplay analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase).** The app uses Google's
Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, so we can fix bugs and
balance the levels. It collects, on our behalf:
- a random **app-instance ID** created by Firebase for this installation (not
  your name, email or Google account);
- **app interactions**: game events such as starting and finishing a level, the
  result, how many attempts it took, boosters and continues used, plus the
  sessions Firebase records automatically (first open, session start, time in
  the app, app updates);
- **device and app information**: device model, operating system version,
  language, app version;
- **approximate location** (country/region/city) that Google derives from your
  IP address. Google Analytics does not store the IP address itself.

We configure Google Analytics for Firebase not to collect the advertising ID
and to keep its ad-personalization signals off. Analytics data is not used by us
to build advertising profiles and is not sold. Google Analytics keeps user- and
event-level data for its default retention period (currently 2 months); after
that only aggregated reports remain.

**Rewarded ads (Google AdMob).** The app can offer optional rewarded video ads:
you choose to watch one, in return for an in-game reward. Ads are served by
Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To load and show ads, measure them and
prevent fraud, the SDK collects and shares with Google and its ad partners:
- your **IP address** (and so your approximate location);
- **device and account identifiers**: the Android **advertising ID** and the
  app-set ID;
- **ad and app interactions**, such as taps and video views;
- **diagnostic information** about the SDK's performance.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and wherever else it is
required, the app first shows Google's consent form, and you choose whether ads
may be personalized.

Google processes this data under its own policies:
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
- AdMob and ads: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
- Firebase privacy and security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## What the app does not contain
The app has **no interstitial or banner ads and no forced ads**, and
(currently) **no in-app purchases**. If we add optional purchases later, we
will update this policy before that version is released.

## Your choices
- **Ads:** every ad is optional. In your device's settings (Settings → Google →
  Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads) you can **reset or delete your advertising
  ID** and opt out of ads personalization. Where the consent form applies, you
  can change your choice at any time with the **Privacy options** button in the
  game's Settings.
- **Deletion:** uninstalling the app deletes your local save and the app-instance
  ID. To request deletion of the analytics data associated with your
  installation, contact us at the email above and we will delete it through
  Google Analytics' user-deletion tools.
- **Questions:** write to contact.weastcoastgames@gmail.com.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children. Ads are limited to content suitable for a
general audience.

## Data security & transfers
Analytics and advertising data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and processed
under Google's security practices, which may involve servers outside your
country.

## Changes
We'll update this page if our practices change; the effective date above
always reflects the current version.
